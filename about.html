About MaterialYardage

MaterialYardage.com is an independent website created and maintained by Siva Kumar U. It provides free calculators and practical reference information for estimating landscaping and construction material quantities.

How the calculators work

The calculators use straightforward geometric volume formulas. They convert between common imperial and metric units and apply approximate bulk-density values only when an estimated weight is requested.

Core formulas

Imperial cubic feet = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in ÷ 12) Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27 Metric cubic meters = length (m) × width (m) × depth (m)

Important limitations

Bulk density varies with moisture, composition, grading and compaction. Calculator results are estimates for planning and should not be treated as supplier-certified weights or quantities. For a purchase, confirm the product's coverage and density with the local supplier.

Editorial approach

MaterialYardage aims to keep formulas transparent, show worked examples and distinguish calculated volume from estimated weight. Pages may be updated when calculator logic, reference values or site features change.

Independent site

MaterialYardage is not affiliated with a specific landscape-material supplier or retailer, and it does not require a supplier recommendation to use the calculators.
