Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. About this website

MaterialYardage.com provides free calculators and written guides for estimating landscape-material volume and approximate weight. The calculators do not require an account and are designed to work without users submitting personal information.

2. Information we collect

We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information or other directly identifying information through the calculators. Measurements entered into a calculator are processed in the visitor's browser and are not submitted to a MaterialYardage server by the calculator.

If you contact us by email, we will hold your message and email address for as long as needed to respond to and keep a record of the correspondence.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, such as pages viewed, approximate engagement and technical information about visits. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may process information such as device, browser and website-interaction data.

Google processes Analytics data under its own policies and terms. We use the Analytics measurement ID G-08MTZ3F4Q1 on this website.

4. Google AdSense and advertising cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize advertising. Depending on applicable settings and user choices, advertising may be based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google states that advertising cookies may be used for ad targeting, reporting and frequency management. Users can control personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings.

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices and choices through Google's advertising and privacy resources.

5. Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be placed by Google Analytics, Google AdSense and other third-party services used on this website. These technologies may help measure website usage, remember preferences, measure advertising performance and deliver or personalize advertisements.

6. Your choices

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. You can also manage Google's personalized advertising choices through Google's Ads Settings. Some website functions or advertising features may be affected if cookies are disabled.

7. Third-party services

This website uses third-party services including Google Analytics, Google AdSense and Google-hosted scripts. These providers may process technical or usage information according to their own privacy policies. The site is hosted on a third-party hosting platform, which may process server-log information such as IP address and request details as part of delivering the website.

8. Data security

The website is served over HTTPS where supported. Because no website can guarantee absolute security, visitors should avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information through public website forms or services.

9. Children's privacy

The website is a general-purpose calculator and reference site and is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, analytics configuration, advertising services or applicable requirements change. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions concerning MaterialYardage.com, please use the details on our contact page and mark your message as a privacy enquiry.
