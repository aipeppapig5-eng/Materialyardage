Sod Calculator Guide

This estimator converts lawn dimensions into square footage and approximate pallet or roll quantities using selectable cutting-waste assumptions.

How the estimate works

Surface area is length × width for a rectangular lawn. The calculator applies the selected waste factor and rounds commercial ordering units upward.

Important assumptions

  • Real lawns often contain curves, trees, beds and narrow sections that increase cutting waste.
  • Farm pallet coverage differs by supplier, so confirm the actual pallet square footage before ordering.
  • Measure the installation area rather than relying only on property dimensions.

Frequently asked questions

How much extra sod should I order?

A simple rectangle can need less extra material than an irregular lawn. Use the calculator's waste options as planning assumptions and confirm with the sod supplier.

Can I use this for curved lawns?

Yes as an estimate, but divide complex shapes into measurable sections for a closer result.

Are pallets standardized?

No. Pallet coverage varies by supplier and sod type, so verify the actual unit coverage.

For the calculation assumptions used on this site, see the MaterialYardage methodology.

What the estimate means

Sod is normally ordered by area rather than cubic volume. The calculator converts the lawn dimensions into square footage or square metres and then estimates the number of pallets or rolls using the package coverage you enter or select.

Worked example

1,000 ft² lawn with 450 ft² per pallet

Divide the measured lawn area by the pallet coverage: 1,000 ÷ 450 = 2.22 pallets. Because sod is delivered in whole pallets or supplier-defined units, the practical order quantity is rounded up, with extra allowance considered for trimming and irregular edges.

Measure before ordering

  • Break irregular lawns into rectangles or other simple shapes and add the areas.
  • Subtract permanent hardscape areas that will not receive turf.
  • Ask the supplier how many square feet a pallet or roll actually covers.
  • Plan installation timing because fresh sod is perishable and should normally be installed promptly.

Why the calculator can differ from a supplier quote

Suppliers may use different pallet sizes, roll dimensions, regional products or minimum-order rules. Their package coverage should take precedence over a generic planning assumption.
