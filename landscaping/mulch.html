Mulch Coverage Calculator Guide

This estimator converts a planting area's dimensions and depth into mulch volume and approximate retail bag quantities.

How the estimate works

Calculate area volume from length × width × depth. The result is converted to cubic yards and can be translated into bag quantities using the selected bag coverage assumption.

Important assumptions

  • Bag volume and coverage differ by product; check the actual bag label.
  • Mulch depth should match the planting application rather than being chosen solely to minimize bag count.
  • Keep mulch away from direct contact with tree trunks and building siding where appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

How deep should mulch be?

Depth depends on the application and material. Use the calculator as a quantity tool and follow local horticultural guidance for the finished depth.

Bulk or bags?

Bulk is often convenient for larger areas, while bags can be useful for small or hard-to-access spaces. Compare actual product volume rather than bag count alone.

Should I order extra?

A small allowance can account for uneven depth, handling and settling. Avoid using excessive depth simply to consume leftover material.

For the calculation assumptions used on this site, see the MaterialYardage methodology.

Choosing a practical mulch depth

Depth depends on the material and the job. A shallow decorative refresh uses less material than a new bed where the goal is stronger soil-surface coverage. Avoid burying plant crowns or tree trunks under excessive mulch.

Worked example

20 ft × 15 ft bed at 3 in depth

The required volume is 75 cubic feet, or about 2.78 cubic yards. If the product is sold in 2 ft³ bags, the unrounded equivalent is 37.5 bags, so a practical order would be 38 bags before any additional site-specific allowance.

Bagged versus bulk mulch

Bulk mulch is commonly priced by cubic yard, while bagged mulch is sold by the labelled bag volume. Compare the package volume rather than the visual size of the bag when deciding which option is more economical.

Installation notes

  • Measure each bed separately when depths differ.
  • Keep mulch away from direct contact with trunks and plant crowns.
  • Expect some settling after installation, particularly with lighter organic products.
  • Use the product label for coverage when the manufacturer publishes one.
