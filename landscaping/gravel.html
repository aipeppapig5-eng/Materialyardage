Gravel & Road Base Calculator Guide

This estimator helps convert aggregate dimensions into cubic yards and approximate weight for gravel, crushed stone and road-base materials.

How the estimate works

Calculate rectangular volume first, then multiply cubic yards by the selected bulk-density assumption to estimate tons. Density is approximate because aggregate moisture, grading and compaction affect delivered weight.

Important assumptions

  • Use the supplier's density or delivered weight information when available.
  • Driveways and compacted base courses may require a design-specific thickness and compaction allowance.
  • Do not assume all gravel products have the same tons-per-yard value.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the tonnage?

It is an estimate based on an assumed bulk density. Actual delivered weight can differ.

Should I add waste?

A modest ordering allowance can cover grading, uneven subgrade and handling losses. Compacted base work may need a separate compaction consideration.

Can this calculate a driveway?

Yes for a preliminary rectangular estimate. Split irregular driveways into sections for better accuracy.
