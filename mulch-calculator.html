Mulch depth guide

Many landscape beds use about 2 to 3 inches of mulch. Deeper layers can be useful for weed suppression and moisture retention, but avoid piling mulch against trunks and stems.

Formula

Cubic feet = length × width × (depth ÷ 12) Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27 Cubic meters = cubic feet × 0.0283168

Worked example

A 20 ft × 15 ft bed at 3 inches requires 75 cubic feet, or about 2.78 cubic yards. If sold in 2 ft³ bags, that is about 38 bags before allowing for waste or settling.

Bulk or bagged mulch?

Bulk mulch is often convenient for larger beds and is normally ordered by cubic yard. Bagged mulch is convenient for small areas and touch-ups; compare the total volume of bags rather than the number of bags alone.

Ordering allowance

Actual coverage can vary because mulch settles and bed measurements may be irregular. Round the order sensibly and confirm the supplier's stated bag volume or bulk measurement.

FAQ

How many 2 cubic foot bags are in a yard?

There are 13.5 bags of 2 cubic feet in one cubic yard mathematically, so a practical order is rounded up to whole bags.

Does mulch weight matter?

Yes. Moisture and material type change weight considerably, so any tonnage shown is an estimate rather than a shipping specification.
