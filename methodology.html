MaterialYardage Calculation Methodology

This page explains the calculation methods used by MaterialYardage so users can reproduce the results and understand their limitations.

Volume calculation

Rectangular beds and areas are treated as length × width × depth. For imperial inputs, depth in inches is converted to feet before calculating cubic feet.

Cubic feet = length (ft) × width (ft) × [depth (in) ÷ 12] Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27 Cubic meters = cubic feet × 0.0283168466

Metric calculation

Cubic meters = length (m) × width (m) × depth (m) Cubic yards = cubic meters × 1.30795062

Weight estimates

Estimated tons are calculated by multiplying cubic yards by an assumed bulk density in tons per cubic yard. Density is an approximate planning value, not a laboratory measurement.

Estimated tons = cubic yards × assumed tons per cubic yard

Rounding

Displayed volume values are rounded for readability. The underlying calculation uses the entered values at full precision. Bag counts are rounded up because a partial bag cannot normally be purchased.

Settling and waste

Material may settle, compact or vary in coverage. Irregular shapes can also make simple rectangular calculations less precise. Users should compare the calculated amount with site conditions and supplier guidance before ordering.

Density uncertainty

Moisture, particle size, composition, compaction and product type can materially change weight. This is why the site labels tonnage as an estimate and recommends checking the supplier's specifications.

Worked checks

For a 20 × 15 ft area at 3 inches, the geometric volume is 75 ft³, which is 2.7778 yd³ and about 2.1235 m³. For a 40 × 10 ft area at 4 inches, the geometric volume is 133.333 ft³, or about 4.9383 yd³ and 3.775 m³.
