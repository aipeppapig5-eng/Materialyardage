Sand calculator guide

Use this estimator for bedding sand, leveling layers and other rectangular applications. Enter the actual depth and adjust the density if your supplier provides a product-specific value.

Cubic feet = length × width × (depth ÷ 12) Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27 Weight = cubic yards × density (tons/yd³)

Density is an estimate

Sand density changes with moisture, grading and compaction. The default value is a planning assumption, not a supplier-certified shipping weight.

FAQ

How deep should bedding sand be?

Follow the installation specification for the application. Paver systems, masonry work and general fill can have different requirements.

Can I use metric dimensions?

Convert the dimensions to feet and inches for this calculator, or use the site's volume calculators for metric-first projects.

How sand volume and weight relate

Sand volume comes from the dimensions of the project. Weight is estimated by multiplying volume by an assumed bulk density. Moisture and grading can materially change bulk density, so a supplier's published value is preferable for a weight-based order.

Worked example

10 ft × 10 ft × 3 in layer

The geometric volume is 25 cubic feet, or about 0.93 cubic yards. A density-based weight estimate should then use the specific sand product's bulk-density assumption.

Common uses

  • Concrete and mortar ingredient planning.
  • Bedding and leveling layers where the specification calls for sand.
  • General fill or landscape applications when the selected sand is suitable.

Ordering check

Ask whether the supplier sells by cubic yard, cubic metre, ton, tonne or bag. The same numerical volume can correspond to different weights across products.
