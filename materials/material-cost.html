Material cost calculator guide

Use this simple estimator to combine a material quantity, unit price, delivery charge and tax. Enter prices from your local supplier rather than relying on hard-coded market prices.

Material cost = quantity × unit price Tax = (material cost + delivery) × tax rate Estimated total = material cost + delivery + tax

Use the correct unit

Quantity and unit price must use the same unit. For example, use 5 cubic yards with a price of $45 per cubic yard, or 100 bags with a price of $6 per bag.

FAQ

Does this include waste?

Enter the final quantity you intend to purchase after applying an appropriate waste or ordering allowance in the relevant material calculator.

No. MaterialYardage does not set or recommend supplier pricing. Enter your own current quotation.
