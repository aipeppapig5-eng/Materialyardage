Topsoil Depth Guide: How Deep Should Topsoil Be?

The right topsoil depth depends on what you are building. Use these ranges as planning guidance, then calculate the volume with the Topsoil Calculator.

Typical topsoil depths

ProjectTypical depthPlanning note
Existing lawn top-dressing¼–½ inUse a thin, even layer so existing grass remains exposed.
New lawn / turf preparation4–6 inUse enough quality soil to establish a workable root zone.
Flower and planting beds6–12 inDepth varies with existing soil and the plants being installed.
Vegetable / raised beds8–12 in+Deeper beds may suit crops with larger root systems.

How depth changes quantity

Multiply length and width in feet by depth in feet, then divide cubic feet by 27.

Cubic feet = length × width × (depth in ÷ 12)
Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27

Example: 20 ft × 10 ft × 6 in = 100 ft³ = about 3.70 yd³ before any allowance.

Ordering allowance

Loose soil can settle after spreading and watering. A planning allowance of roughly 5–10% can help with settling and uneven areas, but it should not replace site measurements or supplier advice.

Measure uneven or irregular ground

For an L-shaped bed, split the plan into two non-overlapping rectangles, calculate each area, then add them. For a curved or tapered bed, divide it into smaller rectangles or triangles and sum the areas. Avoid counting the same strip twice.

If the ground is uneven, measure the intended finished depth at several representative points. An average depth is useful only if those points fairly represent the whole area. Large level changes may need separate zones so a deep section does not distort the estimate for the rest of the bed.

Worked example

Two planting beds measure 12 × 4 ft and 8 × 3 ft. Their combined area is (12 × 4) + (8 × 3) = 72 ft². At 4 inches deep, volume is 72 × (4 ÷ 12) ÷ 27 = 0.89 yd³ before allowance. Confirm whether the project needs topsoil alone or a specified soil blend.

Topsoil planning checklist

  • Measure the area after defining bed edges.
  • Decide whether existing soil will be removed or retained.
  • Use the finished soil depth, not automatically the excavation depth.
  • Check supplier guidance for screened topsoil, blended soil or lawn dressing.
  • Ask whether quoted volume is loose or delivered/settled volume.
