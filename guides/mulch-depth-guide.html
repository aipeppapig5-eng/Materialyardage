Mulch Depth Guide: How Many Inches of Mulch Do You Need?

Mulch depth controls coverage and material cost. Use these planning ranges and the formula below to estimate bulk volume.

Common mulch depth ranges

UseTypical depthPlanning note
Light refresh1–2 inUseful where an existing mulch layer remains.
General landscape beds2–3 inA common range for a new or refreshed layer.
Deeper organic layer3–4 inUse care around stems and trunks.

Mulch quantity formula

Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27

For irregular beds, split the space into rectangles or other simple sections and add the results.

Bulk vs bags

Bulk mulch is usually quoted by cubic yard while retail mulch is sold by bag volume. Check the actual bag volume printed on the package before converting.
