Material Coverage Tables

Coverage is controlled by depth. These tables help you sanity-check a calculator result before ordering.

Square feet covered by 1 cubic yard

DepthCoverage
1 inch324 ft²
2 inches162 ft²
3 inches108 ft²
4 inches81 ft²
6 inches54 ft²
8 inches40.5 ft²
12 inches27 ft²

These are mathematical volume relationships before settling, compaction or installation losses.

How coverage is calculated

Coverage (ft²) = 27 ÷ depth (ft)

At 3 inches, depth is 0.25 ft, so one cubic yard covers 27 ÷ 0.25 = 108 ft².

Bagged material

Bag coverage depends on the volume printed on the bag. A 2 ft³ bag at 3 inches deep covers about 8 ft² before settling and installation losses.
