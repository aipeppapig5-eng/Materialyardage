How coverage is calculated

Coverage (ft²) = 27 ÷ depth (ft)

At 3 inches, depth is 0.25 ft, so one cubic yard covers 27 ÷ 0.25 = 108 ft².

Bagged material

Bag coverage depends on the volume printed on the bag. A 2 ft³ bag at 3 inches deep covers about 8 ft² before settling and installation losses.

Coverage example for a real project

A rectangular bed measuring 20 ft by 12 ft has an area of 240 ft². At a target depth of 3 inches (0.25 ft), volume is 240 × 0.25 = 60 ft³. Divide by 27 to get 2.22 yd³ before any allowance. If you decide to add a 5% planning allowance, 2.22 × 1.05 ≈ 2.33 yd³. The allowance is an example, not a universal rule.
