Material Density Chart: Approximate Tons per Cubic Yard

Density converts a volume estimate into an approximate weight. Actual bulk density varies with moisture, grading, compaction and composition.

Approximate bulk density

MaterialApprox. tons/yd³
Unscreened topsoil1.10
Screened topsoil1.00
Garden soil / blend0.90
Compost0.60
Shredded bark mulch0.40
Wood chip mulch0.45
Pine straw / bark nuggets0.30
Rubber mulch0.60
Pea gravel1.35
River rock1.35
Crushed stone1.40
Crusher run / road base1.50
Decomposed granite1.30

Use the density

Estimated tons = cubic yards × tons per cubic yard

For example, 5 yd³ at 1.40 tons/yd³ gives an estimate of 7.0 tons. This is a planning calculation, not a certified shipment weight.

Why density varies

Moisture, particle size, void space, organic content and compaction can change bulk density. For delivery and freight planning, use supplier data whenever possible.
