Useful formulas

Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27
Cubic meters = cubic feet ÷ 35.3147
Cubic feet = cubic yards × 27

Volume formula with mixed units

Convert all dimensions to the same length unit before multiplying. For a rectangular area in feet with depth in inches:

Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 324

Example: 20 ft × 12 ft × 3 in ÷ 324 = 2.22 yd³. This is equivalent to converting 3 inches to 0.25 feet, calculating cubic feet, then dividing by 27.

For non-rectangular spaces, split the area into manageable rectangles or use an average depth only when it reasonably represents the ground profile. See the topsoil depth guide and gravel driveway guide.
