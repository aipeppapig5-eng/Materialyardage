Useful formulas

Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27
Cubic meters = cubic feet ÷ 35.3147
Cubic feet = cubic yards × 27

For material weight, multiply volume by an appropriate density. Do not use a generic density for a supplier-certified load.
