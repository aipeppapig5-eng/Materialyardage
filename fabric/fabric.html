Before you buy fabric

Use usable width rather than nominal bolt width when selvages cannot be used. Check grain direction, nap, directional prints, pattern repeats, shrinkage, seam allowances and the actual cutting layout. These calculators provide estimates for planning; they do not optimize arbitrary shapes or replace a garment pattern or professional upholstery plan.
