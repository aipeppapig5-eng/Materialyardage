Fabric Unit Converter

Convert common fabric lengths and widths between imperial and metric units. Conversions are exact unit relationships, not estimates of usable fabric.

Convert fabric measurements

Exact conversion factors: 1 inch = 2.54 cm; 1 foot = 12 inches; 1 yard = 36 inches; 1 metre = 100 cm.

Quick reference

  • 1 yard = 3 feet = 36 inches = 0.9144 metres.
  • 1 metre ≈ 1.09361 yards.
  • 1 inch = 25.4 millimetres.
  • Fabric width and length are both linear measurements; this tool does not estimate area or required yardage.
