Upholstery Fabric Calculator

Estimate fabric for rectangular seat tops, cushion faces and boxing strips. Complex furniture shapes and pattern matching require a professional cutting plan.

Estimate fabric for a simple cushion or seat

Included pieces and limitations

This estimate includes two large faces per cushion and a continuous boxing strip based on the perimeter and depth. It is a simplified rectangle-based estimate and assumes all parts can be arranged on the usable width.

It does not calculate piping, zippers, skirts, arm panels, back panels, tufting, complex shapes or directional pattern matching. For a sofa or chair, use a furniture-specific cutting plan or consult an upholsterer.
