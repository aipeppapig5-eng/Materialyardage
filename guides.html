How to use these guides

  1. Measure the project area as accurately as practical.
  2. Choose a depth appropriate for the material and project.
  3. Use the matching calculator to estimate volume or quantity.
  4. Review density, bag-yield and waste assumptions before ordering.
  5. Confirm the final quantity with the supplier for product-specific specifications.

MaterialYardage provides planning estimates. Actual quantities can vary with moisture, grading, compaction, product yield, site shape and installation method.
