Landscaping & Construction Guides

Practical guides to help you choose material depths, calculate quantities, understand coverage and plan an order. Use the calculators for the numbers, then use these guides to understand the assumptions behind them.

  • Mulch Depth GuideRecommended planning depths, bulk volume, bag coverage and ordering allowances.
  • Gravel Driveway GuideDriveway depth, layer planning, cubic-yard calculations and approximate tonnage.

  • Paver Calculator GuideMeasure paving areas, estimate paver counts and allow for cuts and waste.

Reference Resources

  • Material Density ChartApproximate tons per cubic yard for common soil, mulch, gravel and aggregate materials.
  • Conversion TablesUseful cubic-yard, cubic-foot, cubic-meter, length and area conversions.
  • Coverage TablesApproximate area coverage at common material depths.

How to use these guides

  1. Measure the project area as accurately as practical.
  2. Choose a depth appropriate for the material and project.
  3. Use the matching calculator to estimate volume or quantity.
  4. Review density, bag-yield and waste assumptions before ordering.
  5. Confirm the final quantity with the supplier for product-specific specifications.

MaterialYardage provides planning estimates. Actual quantities can vary with moisture, grading, compaction, product yield, site shape and installation method.
