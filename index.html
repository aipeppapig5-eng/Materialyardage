How Many Yards of Material Do You Need?

Accurate volume estimates help you order enough material without unnecessarily over-ordering. Measure the length and width of the area, decide the finished depth, and select topsoil, mulch or gravel. The calculator converts your measurements into cubic yards and cubic meters and provides an estimated weight.

Cubic Yard Formula

For imperial measurements, calculate cubic feet by multiplying length × width × depth in feet. Then divide by 27 to convert cubic feet to cubic yards. If depth is measured in inches, divide the inches by 12 first.

Cubic yards = length × width × (depth ÷ 12) ÷ 27

Using the Metric Calculator

For metric measurements, multiply length in meters × width in meters × depth in meters to obtain cubic meters. The calculator also converts cubic meters to cubic yards for easy comparison with suppliers that sell by the yard.

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should mulch be?

A typical mulch application is about 2 to 3 inches deep. Avoid piling mulch against tree trunks or plant stems.

How much does a cubic yard of topsoil weigh?

A cubic yard of topsoil is often estimated at about 1.1 tons, but actual weight varies with moisture, composition and supplier.

What is the formula for cubic yards?

Multiply length by width by depth in feet, then divide by 27. If depth is in inches, divide it by 12 to convert it to feet first.

How much gravel do I need?

Measure the area length and width and choose the desired depth. The calculator converts the volume into cubic yards and estimates weight using a typical gravel bulk density.
