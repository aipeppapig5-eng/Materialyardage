Free Material Calculators for Every Project

By Material Yardage Editorial & Estimating Team · Updated November 2026

Material Yardage is a free engineering-calibrated estimation platform built for homeowners, landscape contractors, concrete finishers, quilters, and upholsterers. Rather than relying on flat rough averages, every calculator on Material Yardage pairs real cubic volume geometry with supplier-verified bulk material densities, compaction allowances, pattern repeat multipliers, and an integrated multi-zone Bill of Materials (BOM) cost planner.

1. Bulk Soil & Garden Bed Calculators

Accurately ordering bulk soil or retail bags starts with exact length, width, and settled depth. Our garden and soil suite covers bulk cubic yards, metric cubic meters, US tons, and 40-pound or 2-cubic-foot retail bags across key landscaping applications:

  • Topsoil Calculator — Estimates cubic yards, tons (~1.15 tons/yd³ for screened loam), and 40-lb bags for lawn leveling, reseeding, and garden grading.
  • Raised Garden Bed Soil Calculator — Built-in 4×4 and 4×8 bed presets outputting an ideal raised-bed blend of topsoil, organic compost, and perlite/vermiculite aeration.
  • Potting Mix & Planter Volume Calculator — Uses frustum geometry to convert tapered round pots, window boxes, and fabric grow bags into dry quarts and gallons.
  • Compost Calculator — Calculates quarter-inch and half-inch lawn topdressing volume and garden soil amendment ratios.
  • Fill Dirt Calculator — Estimates clean subsoil tonnage and compaction allowances for heavy yard grading, trench backfilling, and hole filling.
  • Lawn Leveling Sand & Topdressing Calculator — Measures washed masonry sand required at 0.25-inch to 1-inch leveling depths without smothering turfgrass.

2. Gravel, Stone & Hardscaping Calculators

Stone aggregates vary significantly in bulk weight depending on particle size, mineral composition, and void space. Using verified quarry densities prevents truckload short-orders and overloaded trailers:

  • Pea Gravel Calculator — Tailored for walkways, dog runs, drainage beds, and play areas using ~1.35 tons per cubic yard.
  • Crushed Stone & #57 Gravel Calculator — Built for driveways, French drains, and shed pads with #57 angular limestone and crusher run density presets.
  • Decomposed Granite (DG) Calculator — Incorporates 15%–20% plate-compaction factors for stabilized pathways and courtyard patios.
  • River Rock & Landscaping Stone Calculator — Estimates tonnage for 1-inch to 3-inch decorative dry creek beds, swales, and border strips.
  • Paver Base & Bedding Sand Calculator — Calculates both the 4-inch to 6-inch compacted gravel base layer and the 1-inch screeded bedding sand layer in one unified estimate.
  • Retaining Wall Block & Gravel Backfill Calculator — Computes wall blocks, capstones, buried base courses, leveling trench gravel, and 12-inch drainage stone chimneys.

3. Mulch, Ground Cover, Lawn & Fencing Tools

Compare bulk cubic yard deliveries against retail bagged mulch, safety-certified playground surfacing, Southern pine straw bales, turf rolls, grass seed rates, and perimeter fence materials:

  • Mulch Calculator — Outputs cubic yards and standard 2-cubic-foot bags for shrub beds and tree rings.
  • Playground Wood Chips & Rubber Mulch Calculator — Incorporates ASTM 6-inch, 9-inch, and 12-inch critical fall-height depth standards.
  • Pine Straw Bale Calculator — Converts bed square footage into standard square bales (~38 sq ft) or longleaf jumbo bales (~65 sq ft).
  • Sod Pallet & Roll Calculator — Converts lawn square footage into 2×5 ft turf rolls and 450 or 500 sq ft pallets with edge-cutting waste allowances.
  • Grass Seed Coverage Calculator — Separates bare-ground new lawn seeding rates from seasonal overseeding across Tall Fescue, Kentucky Bluegrass, Ryegrass, and Bermuda.
  • Fence Post & Concrete Bag Calculator — Estimates line posts, corner posts, 2×4 rails, pickets, drainage gravel, and 50-lb or 80-lb concrete bags from linear footage.
